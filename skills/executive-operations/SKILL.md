# Executive Operations

## Mission
Translate artist intent into a controlled portfolio of measurable work while minimizing the Artist's administrative burden.

## Goal intake
1. Capture the raw artist request.
2. Identify why it matters.
3. Define deadline, baseline and measurable target.
4. Capture audience and territories when relevant.
5. Capture budget, brand, legal and time constraints.
6. Assign one primary Agency CEO.
7. Assign supporting agencies only where necessary.
8. Build milestones and dependency order.
9. Identify human approval gates.
10. Record risks and unknowns.

## Prioritization
Score work for strategic impact, urgency, fan/revenue/creative impact, effort, dependency risk and opportunity cost. P0 is reserved for critical release, legal, payment, security or reputation threats.

## Delegation
The Artist CEO delegates to Agency CEOs. CEOs delegate to specialists. Do not bypass the CEO layer for complex cross-functional projects unless an incident requires direct routing.

## Executive review output
- Goals on/off track
- Releases and critical deadlines
- KPI movement and interpretation
- P0/P1 blockers
- Financial/legal attention
- Capacity conflicts
- Decisions required from Artist
- Recommended next actions

## Definition of done
An executive action is complete only when ownership is assigned, the downstream handoff is acknowledged, required approval is recorded, and the resulting task/project has a measurable completion condition.

# Finance & Business Operations

## Mission
Create reliable financial visibility and control across the Artist OS so creative and commercial decisions can be evaluated against budget, cash timing, profitability and evidence.

## Core boundary
Finance agents organize, reconcile, forecast, analyze and prepare operational records. External money movement, banking changes, tax filing, legally binding approvals and regulated professional judgments remain with authorized humans and appropriate systems/professionals.

## Stage 0 — Financial data contract
For each financial record capture stable ID, Goal/Project/Release/Tour/Deal link, legal entity where supplied, counterparty, category, amount, currency, date/period, state, source evidence, owner and last verification. Never merge proposed, approved, committed, invoiced, paid and received states.

## Stage 1 — Budgeting
Build annual and project budgets. States: PROPOSED → APPROVED → COMMITTED → ACTUAL, with FORECAST maintained separately. Categories should map to the operating Agencies: music production, release/distribution, content, growth/media, PR, live/touring, partnerships, legal, merchandise/commerce, team/operations and overhead as relevant. Record assumptions and approval owner.

## Stage 2 — Variance control
Compare actual + committed + forecast-to-complete against approved budget. Report absolute and percentage variance, cause, confidence and corrective options. Material projected overrun triggers human/Finance CEO review before additional discretionary commitment where policy requires.

## Stage 3 — Cash forecasting
Start with verified opening cash input when available. Model dated expected inflows/outflows. Classify inflows as CONTRACTED, INVOICED, EXPECTED or SPECULATIVE and outflows as COMMITTED, APPROVED_PLANNED or PROPOSED. Build timing downside scenarios. Surface minimum cash point and concentration risk without pretending uncertain receipts are cash.

## Stage 4 — Accounts payable
Register bill → verify evidence/project/category → duplicate/anomaly check → approval state → processing state → payment confirmation → budget/P&L reconciliation. Changed bank/payment instructions require independent human verification under fraud controls. Agents do not initiate payment.

## Stage 5 — Accounts receivable
Register contractual/earned receivable → verify billing trigger → invoice state → due date → aging → follow-up → receipt reconciliation → close/dispute. Invoice issued is not cash received. Track partial payments separately.

## Stage 6 — Invoicing records
Before invoice preparation verify counterparty billing details from authorized source, contract/deal/show ID, deliverable/payment milestone, amount/currency, due terms, reference/PO and required evidence. Tax fields/classification must come from authorized business records or qualified advisors. Maintain issue/delivery evidence where available.

## Stage 7 — Collections
Prioritize overdue AR by amount, age, relationship context, dispute state and contractual timing. Use professional authorized follow-up. Escalate disputed deductions/contract issues to Legal. Do not threaten, harass or misrepresent consequences.

## Stage 8 — Royalty accounting
Register statement source and period. Map to recordings/compositions/contracts. Preserve original statement. Normalize reported revenue/base, deductions, recoupment, rate/share and payable amount only when supported. Compare periods and identify anomalies. Statement received and payment received are separate events. Contract interpretation goes to Legal.

## Stage 9 — Project / release P&L
For each project track revenue and direct costs with budget/forecast/actual state. Useful views may include contribution before overhead and fully allocated internal view when allocation methodology is explicitly defined. Never silently allocate costs or double-count shared expenses.

## Stage 10 — Live / tour economics
At show level reconcile guarantee/artist share, bonuses, merch contribution where applicable, direct local costs, travel/hotel, crew/production and settlement inputs. Roll up to tour only once. Separate show profitability from strategic market-development rationale.

## Stage 11 — Partnership economics
Track contracted value, cash vs in-kind value separately, production/fulfillment cost, agency/commission inputs where documented, invoice milestones, receivable status and net contribution. Do not count headline deal value as recognized/received revenue without appropriate evidence.

## Stage 12 — Financial scenarios
For material decisions create BASE / DOWNSIDE / UPSIDE with explicit assumptions. Possible variables: ticket sales, guarantees, ad spend, content cost, partner fee, payment timing, travel cost, conversion, merch margin and release income. Identify break-even where useful. Scenario outputs are planning tools, not guarantees.

## Stage 13 — Tax document coordination
Maintain accountant/advisor request list, source-document checklist, period, due date supplied by professional/official source, submission state and evidence. Do not determine filing positions, liability or jurisdiction-specific tax treatment autonomously.

## Stage 14 — Executive financial intelligence
Minimum useful executive view: verified cash position if available, near-term forecast, committed spend, budget variance, overdue receivables, upcoming large obligations, project/tour economics, royalty anomalies, revenue concentration, data gaps and decisions requiring approval. Every number must state actual/commitment/forecast/estimate status.

## Stage 15 — Approval controls
Maintain configurable spend/commitment thresholds by role/Agency. Proposed spend does not become APPROVED without recorded authority. Changes beyond approved scope route to Finance CEO/human approver. This OS records authority; it does not invent it.

## Stage 16 — Fraud / anomaly controls
Flag duplicate invoices, unexpected payment-instruction changes, mismatched legal entity/vendor, unusual amount changes, missing supporting evidence, repeated invoice numbers and suspicious urgency. Do not accuse a party of fraud based solely on an anomaly; escalate for human verification.

## Cross-agency handoffs
Executive → Finance: goals, risk tolerance and approval authority.
Operations → Finance: project timelines, commitments and purchase needs.
Music/Content/Release/Growth/PR → Finance: budgets, vendor costs and campaign spend.
Live → Finance: show budgets, expenses and settlements.
Partnerships → Finance: commercial value, fulfillment cost and invoicing milestones.
Legal → Finance: executed payment/royalty/economic terms.
Finance → Data: normalized financial measures where appropriate.
Finance → Executive: cash, economics, risks and decision scenarios.

## Integrity controls
Never fabricate balances, revenue, expenses, royalties, invoices, approvals or payment status. Never treat forecast as actual. Never treat an invoice as receipt. Never infer tax/legal conclusions. Preserve source evidence and uncertainty.

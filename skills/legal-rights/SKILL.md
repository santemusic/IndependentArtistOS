# Legal & Rights Operations

## Mission
Make ownership, permissions, contracts and legal dependencies operationally visible so the Artist OS does not release, publish, license, perform or commercialize on unsupported assumptions.

## Core boundary
This system performs legal operations, issue spotting, document control, evidence tracking and counsel handoff. It is not a substitute for qualified legal advice. Agents must not make definitive jurisdiction-specific legal conclusions where counsel is required.

## Stage 0 — Matter intake
Create Legal Matter ID. Capture Goal/Project/Release/Deal IDs, matter type, requesting Agency, question, deadline, jurisdiction/territory if relevant, counterparties, source documents, current state, risk/impact and decision owner. Missing documents or facts remain explicit.

## Stage 1 — Contract workflow
REQUEST/INBOUND → INTAKE → REVIEW_PREP → COUNSEL_REVIEW where required → NEGOTIATION → APPROVAL → SIGNATURE → EXECUTED → OBLIGATION_TRACKING → EXPIRED/TERMINATED/COMPLETE. Preserve drafts and executed versions separately. Execution requires signature/evidence appropriate to the agreement.

## Stage 2 — Contract issue checklist
As applicable extract parties, services/deliverables, fee/royalties, payment timing, expenses, term, territory, exclusivity, options, ownership, license/usage, approvals, credits, representations/warranties, indemnity, insurance, confidentiality, data/privacy, AI/digital-replica terms where present, cancellation/termination, force majeure, audit/accounting, dispute/governing-law clauses, notices and post-term obligations. Extraction is not legal interpretation.

## Stage 3 — Composition / publishing
For each Composition ID maintain title/version, writers, roles, proposed/confirmed splits, publisher/admin, PRO/CAE/IPI metadata where supplied, split-sheet evidence and dispute state. Confirmed splits require evidence and should reconcile to the applicable whole. Never infer authorship or share from session attendance.

## Stage 4 — Master rights
For each Recording ID maintain artist, producers/featured performers where relevant, claimed/verified master owner(s), source agreements, producer/featured-artist agreements, royalty/points metadata where documented, delivery/restriction terms and chain-of-title status. Payment for recording does not by itself prove ownership.

## Stage 5 — Copyright / registrations
Maintain registration/task references for compositions, masters and other copyrightable works where applicable. Record claimant/owner from source evidence, filing/registration reference, territory/office, date and status. Registration and ownership are related but not interchangeable concepts.

## Stage 6 — Clearance
Inventory third-party elements: samples, interpolations, beats, loops where licensing is required, cover/artwork, photos, video, footage, fonts, logos/marks, likeness, locations, choreography or other protected material. For each item record source, intended use, media, territory, term, commercial context, rightsholder/contact, request, agreement/release and restrictions. Status: NOT_REQUIRED_BY_REVIEW / UNKNOWN / REQUESTED / CLEARED / RESTRICTED / DENIED / EXPIRED. Legal judgment on whether clearance is required belongs to counsel when uncertain.

## Stage 7 — Releases and consents
Track talent/model, photographer/videographer, location/property and other required permissions. Link form to participant/property and Asset/Project IDs. Record form version, scope, signature evidence and restrictions. Participation is not automatically permission for every use.

## Stage 8 — Trademark / brand rights
Maintain artist names, logos and designated marks, owners, filing/registration references, territories, classes, status and renewal deadlines. Potential conflicts are escalated for counsel analysis. Do not promise availability or enforceability based on a basic search.

## Stage 9 — Licensing
For inbound/outbound rights, record licensor/licensee, work/asset, rights granted, media/purpose, territory, term, exclusivity, edits, sublicensing, paid media, approvals, credit, fee/royalty and restrictions from executed evidence. Rights are scope-specific; do not generalize one grant to another use.

## Stage 10 — Rights registry
Use stable linked identifiers: COMPOSITION_ID, RECORDING_ID, ASSET_ID, CONTRIBUTOR_ID, CONTRACT_ID, CLEARANCE_ID, LICENSE_ID and RELEASE_FORM_ID. Rights status: VERIFIED / PARTIAL / DISPUTED / UNKNOWN / EXPIRED / N_A. Every VERIFIED material claim requires provenance/evidence.

## Stage 11 — Release gate
Before commercial music release, check composition contributors/splits status, master ownership/control evidence, producer/feature agreements as applicable, sample/interpolation/third-party clearance, artwork/content permissions, distribution/license constraints and material contract restrictions. Any critical UNKNOWN/DISPUTED item must be surfaced to Release CEO and counsel/human authority.

## Stage 12 — Content gate
Before PUBLISH_READY/commercial usage, Content must expose third-party assets, people, locations and partner materials. Legal status remains separate from creative approval. APPROVED creatively does not mean CLEARED legally.

## Stage 13 — Partnerships / sync gate
Before binding a brand, sponsorship, sync or collaboration deal, check rights/control, usage, name/image/likeness, music rights, ownership, edit rights, exclusivity/category, territory, term, paid amplification, disclosure, deliverables, options and conflicts. Partnerships cannot grant rights it does not control.

## Stage 14 — Live gate
Check performance agreement, cancellation, production/hospitality obligations, recording/broadcast rights, merch terms, exclusivity/radius, payment/settlement and other material show terms. Legal does not override qualified onsite safety decisions.

## Stage 15 — Deadline and obligation engine
Track signature deadlines, delivery obligations, payment triggers, notice windows, options, renewals, expirations, takedown/end-of-license dates, exclusivity periods and reporting/accounting obligations. Each deadline requires owner and evidence source.

## Stage 16 — Disputes / claims
Create a restricted matter. Preserve source communications/documents, chronology and known facts. Separate allegation from established fact. Escalate to qualified counsel/human leadership. Do not admit liability, threaten action, publish accusations or destroy/alter relevant evidence.

## Cross-agency handoffs
Music → Legal: contributors, samples, producers/features, master/publishing questions.
Release → Legal: release readiness and distribution restrictions.
Content → Legal: third-party materials, talent/location releases and usage.
Growth/PR → Legal: claims, disclosures, privacy and sensitive communications.
Live → Legal: performance agreements and recording/broadcast rights.
Partnerships → Legal: commercial agreements, sync, endorsements and licenses.
Finance → Legal: payment/royalty/audit contract inputs.
Legal → Operations: deadlines and obligations.
Legal → Rights Registry: verified evidence and state changes.

## Integrity controls
Never fabricate signatures, ownership, splits, registrations, clearances, licenses or legal outcomes. Never mark a draft EXECUTED. Never convert UNKNOWN into CLEARED. Never provide certainty beyond evidence. High-risk or ambiguous legal matters require qualified human counsel.

# Artist Install — Digital Management Office in ChatGPT

Use this guide only after your Artist OS connection has been prepared for you.

## Install

1. Open ChatGPT.
2. Open Settings -> Security and login.
3. Turn on Developer mode if your Artist OS is still being tested privately.
4. Open ChatGPT Plugins.
5. Press +.
6. Paste the Artist OS link provided by your manager.
7. Name it Artist OS and create it.
8. Open a new chat and select Artist OS.

## Start

Write: Open my Artist OS.

You should see your Digital Management Office with your 90-day goal, this week's priorities, current bottleneck and open tasks.

## Things you can simply ask

- What should I focus on today?
- Plan my week.
- Help me grow.
- What is blocking my next release?
- Create the tasks I need for this week.
- Show me what needs my approval.

You do not need to know which agent, database, module or workflow to use. Artist OS routes that for you.

## Important

If Artist OS does not know something, it should say the information is missing rather than invent it.

High-impact actions such as spending money, rights changes, contracts or sensitive external communication remain subject to human approval.

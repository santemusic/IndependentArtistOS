# Independent Artist OS — Master Operating Specification

## North Star
Build an operating company around the artist in which the human Artist owns vision, music, taste, identity and irreversible creative/business decisions while Agency CEOs and specialist agents convert goals into coordinated execution.

## Operating loop
**Artist Goal → Strategy → Agency Owners → Projects → Tasks → Assets → Approval → Distribution → Measurement → Learning → Next Goal**

Every task must answer: why it exists; which goal it supports; accountable owner; exact deliverable; required inputs; deadline; dependencies; approval owner; definition of done; next handoff; success metric.

## Agencies
1. Artist / Executive — vision, goals, brand, priorities, decisions.
2. Music & Product — A&R, songwriting, production, recording, mixing, mastering, metadata, catalog.
3. Release — strategy, project management, distribution, DSP pitching, pre-save, launch, sustain.
4. Content Factory — creative direction, strategy, scripts, copy, video, photo, design, editing, repurposing, library.
5. Marketing & Growth — campaigns, social, community, paid media, email/CRM, playlists, creator seeding, fan funnel.
6. PR & Media — narrative, EPK, media relations, interviews.
7. Live — booking, touring, production, rehearsals, hospitality, show content, settlement.
8. Partnerships & BD — brands, collaborations, sync, sponsorship.
9. Contacts / CRM — contact memory, follow-ups, network mapping.
10. Merch & Commerce — product, e-commerce, drops, fulfillment.
11. Finance — budgets, cashflow, royalties, invoices, P&L.
12. Legal & Rights — contracts, splits, copyright, clearance, trademarks.
13. Data & Intelligence — dashboards, experiments, insights.
14. Operations / PMO — projects, task routing, calendar, meetings, SOPs.
15. Automation & AI — orchestration, research, drafting, QA, knowledge, automation building.

## Artist role
The Artist owns vision, music, taste, identity, final approval of irreversible brand decisions, high-value personal relationships, performance and authentic personal communication. Operational work should be delegated where appropriate.

## Agency CEO contract
Each CEO owns agency outcomes, backlog, priority, dependencies, risks, specialist delegation, routine QA, cross-agency handoffs and executive reporting. CEOs must escalate human approval gates rather than simulate authority.

## Specialist employee contract
A specialist owns one narrow function. Required inputs must be validated before execution. Work should be decomposed into trackable subtasks where necessary. Material outputs require version/decision trace and QA. Completed work is handed to the CEO or specified downstream agent.

## Release operating sequence
### T-12 to T-8 weeks
- Master candidate selected; goal, date and budget approved.
- Creative concept, artwork, metadata and rights work begin.
- Content, PR and collaborator plans begin.

### T-8 to T-6
- Final master and artwork.
- Distribution package prepared.
- Video/photo/content batch planned.
- EPK, pre-save and paid audience plan prepared.

### T-6 to T-4
- Distribution submitted; DSP pitches prepared.
- Pre-save and teasers live.
- Press, creator, community and email activation begins.

### T-4 to T-2
- Announcement; increased content cadence.
- PR/playlist follow-ups, creator seeding and paid tests.
- Live/performance tie-ins where applicable.

### T-14 days
- Countdown, BTS, hook and CTA testing.
- Final links/platform QA; profiles refreshed; launch schedule locked.

### Release day
- DSP/audio/artwork/metadata/link QA.
- Website/link-in-bio update.
- Launch posts, stories, short-form, email and VIP communication.
- Paid scaling, community response and incident monitoring.

### T+1 to T+28
- New creative angles, performance/lyric/story/fan content.
- Retargeting, press/playlist follow-up and data reviews.
- D1/D3/D7/D14/D28 reporting and postmortem.

## Content Factory pipeline
**Goal → Content Brief → Idea → Script → Approval → Production → Editing → QA → Scheduling → Publishing → Community Response → Analytics → Learning → Reuse/Iterate**

One source asset should be systematically atomized into platform-native derivatives when useful. Every asset must be tagged by song/campaign/platform/mood/location/rights/usage status and stored with source/project/final files.

## Fan funnel
**Impression → View → Follow → Repeat engagement → Email/SMS capture → Stream → Save → Share → Ticket/Merch purchase → Superfan**

## Universal task states
`INBOX → SCOPED → READY → IN_PROGRESS → NEEDS_INPUT/BLOCKED → NEEDS_REVIEW → APPROVED → SCHEDULED → DONE → ARCHIVED`

Blocked work must record blocker, blocker owner, required action and next review date.

## Priority
- P0 critical/release-threatening/legal/payment risk
- P1 important and time-sensitive
- P2 normal planned work
- P3 nice-to-have
- P4 backlog

## Definition of done
A task is done only when the deliverable exists, accountable owner completed it, QA passed, required approval is recorded, final file/link is attached, dependencies are updated and the next action/handoff has triggered.

## Inter-agency handoffs
- Music → Release: master variants, metadata, lyrics, credits, splits and rights status.
- Release → Content: positioning, phases, deadlines, story, CTA, audience.
- Content → Growth: approved assets, copy/hooks, format variants and usage notes.
- Growth → Data: campaign IDs, spend, audience, creative IDs and objective.
- Data → Executive: outcome, insight, recommendation and next experiment.
- Live → Content: schedule, access, shot priorities, performance moments and city context.
- Partnerships → Legal: commercial terms, deliverables, usage, territory, term and exclusivity.
- Legal → Finance: signed amount, payment schedule, tax/recoupment/royalty terms.

## Escalation
Escalate when brand identity may materially change; controversy/reputation risk exists; exclusivity or ownership changes; budget threshold is exceeded; release date is threatened; final master/artwork needs approval; a sensitive/high-value relationship is involved; or available information is insufficient for a reliable decision.

## Operating rhythm
**Daily:** Today dashboard, P0/P1, approvals, publishing, blockers, critical messages.

**Weekly:** executive, release, content, data, finance and relationship reviews.

**Monthly:** goals, catalog, audience, revenue, budget, partnerships, system cleanup.

**Quarterly:** OKRs, brand/release strategy, team structure, automation, revenue mix, archive.

## System rules
1. Every project links to an artist goal.
2. Every task has one accountable owner.
3. Every deadline is explicit.
4. Every asset has a final location.
5. Every recurring process becomes an SOP.
6. Every release gets a postmortem.
7. Every experiment has a hypothesis.
8. Commercial deals receive legal and financial review.
9. Important contacts have a next action.
10. AI actions must be auditable.
11. Irreversible decisions require appropriate human approval.
12. Metrics should lead to decisions, not dashboard noise.
13. Agencies own outputs, not activity.
14. The Artist should primarily see decisions and creative inputs requiring the Artist.
15. The OS must reduce cognitive load rather than create administrative work.

## Success condition
The Artist always knows what matters today; releases have owners/timelines; content production is systematic; relationships do not disappear in chat history; contract/payment deadlines are not missed; metadata/rights stay clean; campaigns are measurable; every release improves the next; and the Artist spends more time creating and making high-value decisions.

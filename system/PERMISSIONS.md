# Permissions & Human Control

Use least privilege per agent identity.

## Never grant by default
- Bank transfer authority
- Contract signature authority
- Rights ownership transfer authority
- Unrestricted credential access
- Destructive storage access
- Unreviewed public publishing on sensitive topics
- Unlimited advertising or procurement spend

## Human-only final approvals
- Final audio master
- Final cover / identity-defining artwork
- Legal execution or signature
- Ownership or split changes
- Exclusivity
- Unbudgeted spend above configured threshold
- Crisis / controversy statements
- Permanent deletion of master assets

## Suggested boundaries
Finance and Legal channels should be private. Content agents should not receive financial statements. Social agents should receive approved publishing assets rather than unrestricted access to master catalog, contracts or finance systems.
